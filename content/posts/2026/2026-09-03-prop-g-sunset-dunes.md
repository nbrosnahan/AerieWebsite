## More ballot propositions to nowhere

The carbrained people and politicians are once again trying to re-litigate Prop K.
They so desperately want their highway back that they're going to lie about it.

They make the bold claim in their marketing: "Yes on G!" "SAFE STREETS & GREAT PARKS" "SF NEEDS BOTH!"
Oh yes, let's close a new beloved park and open a tired old highway.  That's the ticket.
Also, it'll cost $10M to remove the park and an additional $2M to maintain the road every year.

Let's be clear.  Cars never make parks great, and they absolutely do not make streets safe.

I love walking and riding in Sunset Dunes Park.  The creation of the park hasn't caused the traffic apocalypse that everyone feared.
This effort to un-ring the park bell is silly.  Once again.

Vote No on G.  The park is here to stay and your car can go somewhere else please.
