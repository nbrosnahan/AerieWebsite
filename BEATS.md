# Beats

Topics the weekly idea generator searches against. Edit freely — add, remove,
reorder, or scribble notes under a beat. The generator reads this file at the
start of every run and treats each `## ` heading as one beat to research.

Bullet points under a beat are hints: specific orgs, projects, corridors,
people, or angles worth watching. They are not required.

Beats marked `(paused)` in the heading are skipped.

---

## SF & Bay Area urbanism

- Fresh news from the past week, grounded in actual items with links
- SFMTA board actions, quick-builds, street redesigns
- Public space: parklets, plazas, Slow Streets, JFK Promenade
- Neighborhood-level change in the parts of SF I actually walk

## Cycling advocacy

- SF Bicycle Coalition, Bike East Bay, SVBC
- Protected bike lane projects and the fights over them
- Valencia, the Great Highway, Oak/Fell, the Wiggle
- Bike infrastructure I've ridden and can speak to firsthand

## Transit

- Muni, BART, Caltrain, regional rail
- Funding measures and operating shortfalls
- Service changes, fare policy, the Link21 / Caltrain electrification arc

## Housing

- SF and state-level housing policy, upzoning, the Housing Element
- CEQA reform, permitting, builder's remedy
- Concrete projects going up (or not) in SF

## Street safety data & Vision Zero

- Collision and fatality data, and what the numbers say before/after a redesign
- SFPD traffic enforcement levels, automated speed enforcement, daylighting
- SF Vision Zero benchmarking data, civil grand jury reports, SFMTA evaluations
- Before/after stats on projects I've ridden — the Fell/Oak collision drop kind of thing

## Ideas worth importing

- Dutch, Danish, Parisian, and UK street design worth stealing outright
- Mobility data and comparison tools (citiesmoving.com and its kin)
- Books and reports on car dependence, and what they get right or overstate
- Small physical interventions — fietsvlonders, modal filters, bike parking — that fit an SF block

## Tech, DIY & home projects

- Home automation, IoT, and the over-engineered fix to a minor irritation
- Hardware hacks and teardowns, especially where the vendor's failure mode is dumb
- Side-projects, self-hosting, and this site's own plumbing
- Backend/systems takes on consumer tech that was clearly designed by nobody

## Ballot measures & campaign spin

- Local and state measures on the current ballot, and who is funding each side
- Campaign mailers and slogans that say the opposite of what the measure does
- Following a measure from signature gathering through implementation
- What passed and whether the thing voters were promised actually got built

## Books, games & screen stuff

- Books on cities, transport, and technology, and what they get right or overstate
- Video games worth the time, especially co-op and roguelikes
- Films, trailers, and TV worth a short note
- Long-form speculative pieces and scenario writing about technology
